## Category

Structara is an evidence-first knowledge compiler. It is not a generic OCR
tool, chat wrapper, file organizer, or decorative knowledge graph.

## Signature experience

**Editorial Intelligence 2.0 — Evidence in Motion.** A visitor sees a real
source enter the system, become typed structure, pass or fail explicit
verification, form a knowledge package, and remain linked to exact source
evidence. The product is the hero; first-party brand objects support it.

## Visual style

- Warm paper and carbon ink establish editorial authority.
- Electric blue marks selection, routing, and user action.
- Evidence cyan is reserved for verified source links and geometry.
- Amber marks review; red marks failure or quarantine. Neither is decoration.
- Dense product detail sits inside generous editorial whitespace.
- Hairlines, coordinates, receipts, and measured transitions make computation
  legible without turning the interface into a sci-fi control panel.

## Must feel

- Exact, calm, expensive, inspectable, and technically ambitious.
- Immediately useful at three seconds, credible at ten seconds, and complete
  enough to evaluate at sixty seconds.
- Like a premium enterprise instrument with a strong editorial point of view.
- Alive because evidence moves through real states, not because decoration
  drifts in the background.

## Must never feel

- Like a generic SaaS bento grid, purple gradient template, glass dashboard,
  AI orb, node-cloud wallpaper, or empty wireframe.
- Like benchmark theatre: every number needs dataset, sample count, evaluator,
  environment, date, and evidence state.
- Like internal QA: implementation labels, truth-class jargon, and provisional
  status text stay out of customer copy unless they explain a real limitation.
- Like an autoplay ad: motion is controllable, silent, pausable, and removed or
  reduced when the user requests less motion.

## Product truth hierarchy

1. T0 verified product or public evidence.
2. T1 deterministic product visualization derived from a frozen fixture.
3. T2 first-party brand object, always visibly illustrative.
4. T3 generated editorial media, never used as proof.
5. T4 rights-cleared external media with provenance.

No lower tier may visually imply a higher tier. Unmeasured values render as
unavailable, not estimated or filled with marketing copy.

## Signature scenes

1. Drop Everything: collection intake, classification, dedupe, and policy.
2. Source to Structure: one source page becoming typed blocks.
3. Evidence Link: one number returning to its exact cell or bbox.
4. First Verified: parallel candidates resolving through hard gates.
5. Knowledge Package: notes, entities, relations, and portable exports.
6. Measured Benchmark: accuracy, reliability, latency, and cost with repeat
   distributions and failure counts.

## Composition rules

- One dominant product scene per viewport.
- Marketing layout may be asymmetric; product controls remain systematic.
- Each section must advance the story, not restate the previous section in a
  different card grid.
- Charts include visible values and a table alternative.
- Mobile keeps the proof moment and primary control above decorative support.

## Motion character

Transitions are evidence handoffs: collect, classify, route, verify, connect,
package. UI feedback uses restrained ease-out motion; cinematic sequences use
linear or gentle ease-in-out timing. No overshoot, spring bounce, cursor chase,
or infinite ambient movement. See `MOTION_MANIFEST.yml`.

## Release bar

The visual system is not complete until the 1920, 1440, 1024, 390, and reduced
motion captures pass; critical/high findings are zero; all controls work; all
claims are evidence-bound; and current-worktree captures replace stale assets.
