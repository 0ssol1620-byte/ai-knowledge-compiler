# Terms of Service — Draft Outline

> Counsel must replace every bracketed field and approve the final localized
> agreement. This outline is not publishable legal text.

## 1. Service and eligibility

Identify the contracting entity, effective date, eligible users, authority to
bind an organization, account security, and supported service plans.

## 2. Customer content and rights

The customer retains ownership and grants only the limited rights needed to
host, secure, process, reproduce, and export submitted content. The customer
warrants sufficient rights and must not upload unlawful, infringing, malicious,
or unauthorized personal data.

## 3. AI processing and accuracy

Explain extracted, reconstructed, summarized, inferred, and user-edited layers;
source evidence and quality warnings; model/provider changes; and that outputs
can contain errors and are not legal, medical, financial, or other professional
advice.

## 4. External processing and training

Customer content is not used for training by default. External Precision
providers and training use require a separate, informed opt-in. Private
processing forbids external provider egress.

## 5. Credits, payment, and refunds

Define reservation, consumption, release, failed-page refund, reversal,
expiration, tax, payment failure, subscription change, storage overage, and
transparent Precision surcharges. A provider retry is not a second charge.

## 6. Retention and deletion

State plan-specific source/result retention, customer deletion controls,
backup-expiry behavior, deletion receipts, legal hold, and limitations on
removing influence from an already deployed model after an optional training
opt-in.

## 7. Security, privacy, and subprocessors

Incorporate the Privacy Notice and DPA as applicable. Describe reasonable
controls without promising absolute security or residency that the selected
provider does not guarantee.

## 8. Acceptable use and enforcement

Cover abuse, automated free-plan use, malware, rights violations, credential
sharing, reverse engineering where lawful, reselling access, service disruption,
and notice/appeal where appropriate.

## 9. Suspension, termination, exports, and survival

Provide a reasonable export window, account termination process, payment
settlement, deletion schedule, and surviving provisions.

## 10. Warranty, liability, indemnity, disputes

Counsel supplies jurisdiction-specific warranty disclaimers, liability caps,
indemnities, governing law, dispute process, consumer exceptions, assignment,
changes, notices, severability, and entire-agreement terms.
