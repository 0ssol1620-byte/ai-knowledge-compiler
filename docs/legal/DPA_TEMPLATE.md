# Data Processing Addendum — Draft Checklist

Do not publish this checklist as a signed DPA.

1. Parties, roles, services, term, and documented processing instructions.
2. Subject matter, duration, nature, purpose, data types, and data-subject
   categories.
3. Confidentiality and personnel access controls.
4. Technical and organizational measures, including encryption, tenant
   isolation, audit, backup, secure deletion, incident response, and testing.
5. Subprocessor authorization, current list, notice period, and objection
   process.
6. Cross-border transfer regions and legal transfer mechanisms.
7. Assistance with rights requests, DPIAs, regulator inquiries, and incidents.
8. Incident notification channel, content, timing, and cooperation.
9. Return/deletion on termination, backup expiry, legal hold, and deletion
   evidence.
10. Audit evidence, certifications, confidentiality, frequency, and cost.
11. Training-use prohibition unless separately and explicitly opted in.
12. External Precision provider disclosure and zero-retention commitments when
    purchased.
13. Precedence, liability linkage, governing law, and signatures.
