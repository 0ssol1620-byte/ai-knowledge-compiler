# TAVONEL usability research plan

This protocol is ready to run when the owner supplies participants and consent.
No synthetic participant result may be reported as research evidence.

## Participants

- 4 individual researchers or knowledge workers
- 4 document-operations, compliance, or review specialists
- 4 developers or AI/RAG engineers
- 3 enterprise administrators or security stakeholders

Recruit participants who process complex PDFs, filings, research papers, or
course material. Record role, document frequency, review responsibility, and
current tools. Do not collect source documents containing personal or regulated
data for the public demo workspace.

## Tasks

1. Explain what TAVONEL does after viewing only the homepage hero.
2. Find and verify one number in the public DART demo.
3. Upload a permitted sample and understand the preflight estimate.
4. Follow a Markdown block back to its source bounding box.
5. Resolve a numeric mismatch and a table difference.
6. Split a note, inspect backlinks, and verify a graph relation.
7. Configure and preview an export package.
8. Change a retention or security policy and explain its impact.
9. Choose a pricing plan using the page/scan/Precision estimator.

## Success criteria

- 80% correctly describe “Knowledge Compiler” without a moderator hint.
- 90% can return a result to source evidence.
- 90% distinguish a public fixture from a benchmark quality claim.
- 85% complete first upload and preflight without assistance.
- 85% understand that Precision and storage can change operating cost.
- zero participant mistakes a roadmap item for a currently certified control.

## Evidence

Capture completion, time-on-task, critical errors, assistance count, confidence,
and a short comprehension answer. Link every finding to route, viewport,
participant class, and recording consent. Aggregate only after the minimum
sample is complete.

## Owner actions

The owner must approve recruitment, consent language, incentive, recording
policy, and any customer/company outreach. Until then the research gate remains
prepared, not completed.
