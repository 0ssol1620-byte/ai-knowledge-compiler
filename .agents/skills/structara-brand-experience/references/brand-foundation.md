# Brand foundation

- Category: Knowledge Compiler
- Essence: Trusted structure for intelligent systems.
- Promise: preserve structure, connect evidence, expose uncertainty, keep
  knowledge portable, and distinguish source from generated content.
- Voice: confident but bounded, concise, concrete, evidence-led.
- Primary line: “Your AI is only as good as the knowledge it receives.”
- Product line: “Turn documents into structured, verified, connected
  knowledge.”
