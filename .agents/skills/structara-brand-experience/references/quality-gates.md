# Quality gates

- WCAG 2.2 AA; no serious or critical automated finding.
- No horizontal overflow, visible clipping, console error, or broken asset.
- Reduced motion carries identical information.
- Marketing Lighthouse targets: Performance 90, Accessibility 100, Best
  Practices 95, SEO 95.
- Visual rubric requires 90/100 or higher with no Critical or High issue.
- Never represent lab measurements as field performance.
