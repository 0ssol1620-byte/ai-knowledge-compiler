# Visual language

Use warm canvas `#F5F3EE`, paper `#FCFCFA`, ink `#101216`, graphite
`#34383F`, brand blue `#315BE8`, and evidence cyan `#2AA8BD`. Neutral surfaces
should occupy roughly 82% of a composition.

Marketing uses 12 columns, a maximum width of 1440px, large type, real product
canvases, and alternating editorial structures. Product uses a 4px spacing
system, 240px sidebar, 48px bars, hairline panel boundaries, and 8–10px
radii.

The five glyphs are Page, Block, Link, Node, and Branch.
