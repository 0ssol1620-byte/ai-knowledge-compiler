# Sourcing and license

Prefer owned product/public evidence. CC0 materials and HDRIs may support
custom 3D, but signature objects remain first-party. Open-license and stock
media require source, creator, license URL, commercial and modification rights,
attribution, trademark/person/property review, download date, evidence, hash,
allowed surfaces, and review date.

Never ingest search-result images, Pinterest, competitor UI, social reposts,
unknown-license models, or files without license evidence.
