# H1-J Amendment 1 — four ranking elements could not be ablated alone

**Raised after seeing which controls failed to separate, before any of their
nulls was cited.** The protocol is unchanged. What is added is the reason four
filters produced no separation, and a mechanical proof where one exists.

## The result that needs explaining

| verdict | filters |
|---|---|
| ESSENTIAL | F4, G1, R4, R5, R7, R8 |
| REDUNDANT_BUT_LOAD_BEARING | F1, F2, F3 |
| **UNRESOLVED — control did not separate** | **R1, R2, R3, R6** |

Protocol §6 makes an unseparating control fatal to that filter's null. These
four are reported as **unmeasured**, not as inert.

## Why R1, R2 and R3 could not separate

Ranking elements 1-3 restate admissibility filters F1-F3. With F1-F3 enabled the
offending claim is **already removed from the candidate set**, so there is
nothing for the ranking element to order and disabling it changes nothing.

Ablating R1 meaningfully would require disabling F1 at the same time — which
ablates two mechanisms at once and measures neither. **This instrument cannot
separate them**, and saying so is the honest result. It would take a different
design (a filter-pair ablation with its own protocol) to answer the question.

## Why R6 could not separate — and this one is provable

`scope_match` returns `-1` on any dimension mismatch, and otherwise returns the
count of matched dimensions, which is `len(scope)`. `specificity` is *defined* as
`len(scope)`.

> For every claim admissible under F3 (`scope_match >= 0`), ranking element 3
> and ranking element 6 carry **the same value**. They differ only when
> `scope_match` is `-1`, which F3 already disqualifies.

Verified over probes with 0, 1, 2 and 3 scope dimensions plus a mismatched
control, in the receipt's `structural_analysis` block.

**This is not a measurement failure. It is a finding.** Element 6 is dead weight
in the ranking tuple for every claim that reaches it.

## Claim consequence — binding under protocol §8

- The claim **may not recite specificity as an independent ranking element**
  contributing information beyond scope match. That limitation is narrowed.
- R1, R2 and R3 are recorded as **NOT_MEASURED**. They may not be recited as
  independently validated, and they may not be called redundant either — the
  experiment failed to test them, which is a different statement from testing
  them and finding nothing.

## What did survive, and is the substantive result

**F1, F2 and F3 are redundant-but-load-bearing**, which is the outcome protocol
§3 predicted and constructed for. Each is masked in the `paired` state by the
ranking element that restates it, and each becomes the *only* refusal in the
`solitary` state where no compliant alternative exists. A naive one-at-a-time
ablation would have reported all three as doing nothing.
