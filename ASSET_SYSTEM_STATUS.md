## Source strategy decision

Owned product/public proof, code/SVG, and first-party procedural 3D cover the
current launch surfaces. External search/download was intentionally not used:
it would add provenance and stock-aesthetic risk without answering a missing
page question. The previously generated T3 material stills were rejected and
removed from the branch.

## Production-equivalent decisions

- The procedural TypeScript/R3F scene is the source master for launch. A Blender
  `.blend` is not required by the runtime and would duplicate geometry without
  improving the shipped result.
- Responsive server-rendered CSS composition is the LOD2 poster source. It
  avoids a raster LCP dependency and remains complete with JavaScript disabled.
- Live deterministic interaction and static product evidence replace decorative
  product video on the launch web experience. Recording derivatives can be
  produced from the capture script when a campaign or sales channel is approved.
