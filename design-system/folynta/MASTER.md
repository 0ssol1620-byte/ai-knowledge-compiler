## Brand contract

- Name: `FOLYNTA`
- Category: `The Knowledge Compiler`
- Tagline: `From every page, a system of knowledge.`
- Legal state: `clearance-pending`
- Do not encode the display brand in database keys, object roots, event types, or export schema IDs.

## Visual grammar

- Calm editorial precision, warm matte paper, near-black ink.
- Cobalt marks transformation; cyan marks evidence/provenance.
- Amber is limited to quarantine/review. Red is limited to destructive/error states.
- No aurora, purple gradient, atmospheric fog, bloom, chrome, ornamental glass, particle field, bento card wall, or generic AI hero.
- Use sans typography for interface and display. Use tabular numerals for metrics and receipts.

## Landing composition

Exactly seven narrative scenes:

1. Hero: 5-column copy / 6-column compilation scene; no metric.
2. Full-bleed proof: actual selectable source cell synchronized with result and evidence.
3. Compiler transformation: Understand / Verify / Connect / Activate.
4. Knowledge system: vault tree, atomic note, local graph, and export from one fixture.
5. Quality and routing: measured subsets only, with limits and methodology link.
6. Security and control: Browser → Quarantine → Verified Source → Isolated Worker → Derived Knowledge → Purge, plus policy rail.
7. One final CTA: personal path and enterprise path.

## Motion

- Hero sequence settles once over 8.2 seconds; never loops automatically.
- Pause when hidden or offscreen; render on demand; provide replay after settle.
- No spring overshoot. Camera motion remains within 4 degrees.
- UI transitions are 150–280 ms with non-elastic easing and only `transform`/`opacity` where possible.
- Reduced motion shows the deterministic poster with no WebGL initialization.

## Interaction and accessibility

- Full keyboard access, sequential headings, visible focus, skip link.
- Minimum 44×44 px interactive target.
- Text contrast at least WCAG AA; never communicate state with color alone.
- No hover-only action. Hover effects only under fine-pointer media queries.
- Test 375, 768, 1024, and 1440 px plus reduced motion.

## Evidence discipline

- Product UI, public filing fixtures, benchmark values, security claims, and customer proof must never be generated visual fiction.
- Every benchmark value includes dataset scope, evaluator, hardware/runtime context, limitations, and evidence link.
- `SHADOW`, `IMPLEMENTED-LOCAL`, and `EXTERNAL-BLOCKED` remain visible states; none are promoted to `EVIDENCED` by copy.
