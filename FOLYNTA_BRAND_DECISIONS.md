# Approved decisions

## D-001 — Brand surface

Marketing uses a warm editorial canvas with controlled dark cinematic sections.
Product surfaces use calm, dense, source-first precision.

Status: Approved

## D-002 — Category and promise

FOLYNTA is the Knowledge Compiler for AI. It turns source documents into
structured, verified, connected, and portable knowledge.

Status: Approved

## D-003 — Hero composition

The hero uses asymmetric editorial copy and a page-to-knowledge scene. The
static HTML/CSS poster is complete on its own; React Three Fiber progressively
enhances capable desktop devices.

Status: Approved

## D-004 — Visual system

Warm paper, ink, computational grids, evidence lines, and eighteen structural
glyphs are the system. Brand blue marks transformation; evidence cyan is
reserved for provenance.

Status: Approved

## D-007 — Asset authority

Production asset order is actual product/public proof, deterministic code/SVG,
first-party procedural 3D, approved AI editorial, then licensed external media.
Generated imagery never represents product, benchmark, customer, logo,
certification, security, or public-document evidence.

Status: Approved

## D-005 — Motion

Marketing motion explains transformation. Product motion is event-driven,
brief, and never decorative. Reduced motion carries identical information.

Status: Approved

## D-006 — Claims

No customer logo, testimonial, certification, benchmark score, or absolute
performance claim appears without registered evidence.

Status: Approved
